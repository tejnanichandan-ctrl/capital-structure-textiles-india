# Figure Catalogue

All figures are generated by `R/05_report_figures.R` (report figures) and `R/06_additional_figures.R` (additional figures). They are saved as 300-dpi PNGs, ready to paste into Word.

The "Where it fits" column refers to the section numbers of the written report.

## A note on Figure 2 vs A01
Figure 2 in the report puts D/E and ROE on two different y-axes. With a dual axis, where the two lines cross is decided only by how each axis is scaled, so a reader can see a relationship that isn't in the data. **A01** shows the same series as two stacked panels on a shared year axis, with the COVID years shaded. It is the recommended replacement.
